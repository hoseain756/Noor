# NOVA product

## Register

product

## Users
Arabic and English speaking viewers of all ages, using phones and tablets in portrait or landscape. Parents manage a protected Kids area.

## Product Purpose
Discover movies, series and anime from installed addons, connect personal tracking libraries, choose a source and continue watching. Preserve existing playback, cloud sync and integration capabilities.

## Brand Personality
Cinematic, warm, confident and simple, from the supplied NOVA identity. In a dim room a viewer chooses a story with one hand: the dark Ink canvas keeps artwork central; daylight viewing has a fully designed light option.

## Anti-references
Clutter, nested decorative cards, heavy repeated blur, generic dashboards and imitation of other streaming brands.

## Design Principles
Use original supplied logo art. One clear primary action. Reveal advanced controls progressively. Keep personal libraries intact. Make language and direction changes immediate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA text contrast, 48dp controls, Arabic and English, logical layout properties, large text and reduced motion.
